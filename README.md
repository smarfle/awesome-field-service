## Running the business

- [SBA business guide](https://www.sba.gov/business-guide) - Plan, launch, manage and grow a small business, from the U.S. Small Business Administration.
- [IRS small business and self-employed](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed) - Federal taxes, deductions, estimated payments and forms.
- [IRS worker classification](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/independent-contractor-self-employed-or-employee) - How the IRS tells an employee from an independent contractor.
- [IRS standard mileage rates](https://www.irs.gov/tax-professionals/standard-mileage-rates) - The current cents-per-mile rate for business driving.
- [OSHA small business](https://www.osha.gov/smallbusiness) - Free safety resources and the confidential on-site consultation program.
- [OSHA heat exposure](https://www.osha.gov/heat-exposure) - Preventing heat illness for crews that work outside.

## Pest control

- [EPA pesticides](https://www.epa.gov/pesticides) - Federal pesticide registration, labels and safety.
- [EPA applicator certification standards](https://www.epa.gov/pesticide-worker-safety/certification-standards-pesticide-applicators) - Federal rules behind state certified applicator licenses.
- [AAPCO](https://aapco.org) - Association of American Pesticide Control Officials, with links to state pesticide regulatory agencies.
- [NPIC](https://npic.orst.edu) - National Pesticide Information Center, plain answers about pesticide products and safety.
- [NPMA](https://www.npmapestworld.org) - National Pest Management Association, the industry's trade association.

## HVAC

- [EPA Section 608](https://www.epa.gov/section608) - Refrigerant handling rules and technician certification.
- [ACCA](https://www.acca.org) - Air Conditioning Contractors of America, standards such as Manual J and contractor training.

## Plumbing

- [PHCC](https://www.phccweb.org) - Plumbing-Heating-Cooling Contractors Association.
- [EPA WaterSense](https://www.epa.gov/watersense) - Water-efficient fixture labels and programs to recommend to customers.

## Electrical

- [NFPA 70, National Electrical Code](https://www.nfpa.org/70) - The code most states and cities adopt for electrical work.
- [OSHA electrical safety](https://www.osha.gov/electrical) - Hazards and standards for electrical work.

## Lawn care and landscaping

- [NALP](https://www.landscapeprofessionals.org) - National Association of Landscape Professionals, safety training and certifications.
- [EPA pesticides](https://www.epa.gov/pesticides) - Lawn treatments that use pesticides fall under the same federal and state rules as pest control.

## Cleaning

- [ISSA](https://www.issa.com) - The worldwide cleaning industry association, with training and the CIMS standard.
- [ARCSI](https://www.arcsi.org) - Association of Residential Cleaning Services International, for house cleaning companies.
- [EPA selected disinfectants](https://www.epa.gov/pesticide-registration/selected-epa-registered-disinfectants) - EPA-registered disinfectants for specific germs.

## Pressure washing

- [PWNA](https://pwna.org) - Power Washers of North America, training, certification and environmental guidance.
- [UAMCC](https://www.uamcc.org) - United Association of Mobile Contract Cleaners.
- [EPA stormwater program](https://www.epa.gov/npdes/npdes-stormwater-program) - Background on wash water and storm drain rules; your city may add its own.

## Tree service

- [TCIA](https://www.tcia.org) - Tree Care Industry Association, safety programs and accreditation.
- [ISA](https://www.isa-arbor.com) - International Society of Arboriculture, arborist certification.
- [OSHA tree care](https://www.osha.gov/tree-care) - Hazards and controls for tree trimming and removal.

## Garage doors

- [IDA](https://www.doors.org) - International Door Association, dealer and installer training.
- [DASMA](https://www.dasma.com) - Door and Access Systems Manufacturers Association, technical data sheets and standards.

## Car wash

- [International Carwash Association](https://www.carwash.org) - The car wash industry's trade association.
- [Mobile detailing reply templates](https://github.com/cometrixshop-hash/mobile-detailing-reply-templates) - 20 free, copy-paste text replies for car detailers, from the first "how much?" message to booking confirmations, follow-ups and rebooks (CC0).

## Security and alarm

- [ESA](https://esaweb.org) - Electronic Security Association, training and state association links.
- [TMA](https://tma.us) - The Monitoring Association, alarm monitoring standards and false alarm work.
- [NFPA 72](https://www.nfpa.org/72) - National Fire Alarm and Signaling Code.

## Remodeling and contracting

- [NAHB](https://www.nahb.org) - National Association of Home Builders, including remodeler resources.
- [EPA lead renovation, repair and painting](https://www.epa.gov/lead/renovation-repair-and-painting-program) - Lead-safe certification for work on homes built before 1978.

## Pool service

- [PHTA](https://www.phta.org) - Pool and Hot Tub Alliance, operator and technician certification.

## Home inspection

- [ASHI](https://www.ashi.org) - American Society of Home Inspectors, standards of practice.
- [InterNACHI](https://www.nachi.org) - International Association of Certified Home Inspectors, training and standards.

## Mold and water damage

- [EPA mold](https://www.epa.gov/mold) - Mold cleanup and prevention guidance for homes and buildings.

## Free templates and toolkits

- [Free field service templates](https://smarfle.github.io/field-service-templates/) - 24 Google Sheets and 13 Google Docs for estimates, invoices, schedules, work orders, payroll and service agreements, each explained on its own page.
- [Smarfle Field Service Library](https://sites.google.com/view/smarfle-field-service-library) - A toolkit page per trade with first-year advice, templates and common questions.
